# WaveType Code of Conduct

WaveType welcomes people who want to use, improve, and discuss local dictation. Everyone participating in this project deserves a respectful, constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and professional in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and observable behavior rather than on people.
- Make room for different levels of experience and different perspectives.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and help resolve disagreements.
- Protect other people's privacy, including recordings, transcripts, contact details, and unpublished security information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language aimed at a person or group.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexualized comments or imagery.
- Publishing or seeking someone else's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to WaveType's repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is representing WaveType in an official capacity elsewhere.

## Reporting a concern

Email the project maintainer at [a.bhattacharya.010805@gmail.com](mailto:a.bhattacharya.010805@gmail.com) with the subject **WaveType Code of Conduct report**. Include links or screenshots, dates, and a description of what happened if you can. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports as privately as practical, consider the context and impact, and share details only as needed to address the concern. Good-faith reports will not be penalized. If the concern involves the maintainer or conduct on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's **Report abuse** controls.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may ask someone to change their behavior, edit or remove project content, issue a warning, limit participation temporarily, or ban participation. The response will depend on the severity, history, and impact of the behavior. Immediate restrictions may be used when needed to protect participants.
